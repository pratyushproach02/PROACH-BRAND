Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the site.

Before publishing, replace:
- YOUR PHONE
- YOUR EMAIL@example.com
- project names/photos/details
- exact years of operation (the template currently says 35+)
- product brands/specifications
- address and business registration/contact details
